# WGS CRM Operations

1. Run `wgs-source-check` and read `docs/wgs/06-growth/WGS-CRM-OPERATIONS.md`.
2. Confirm Google Sheets or another explicitly approved system of record before writing.
3. Use the documented lifecycle stages and required fields without inventing parallel labels.
4. Keep one accountable owner and one dated next action for every active record.
5. Record source, consent or lawful basis where relevant, last contact, and outcome.
6. Store only data needed for a legitimate studio purpose.
7. Deduplicate before creation and preserve useful history during merges.
8. Treat revenue forecasts as estimates and show their basis.
9. Ask for explicit approval before bulk imports, destructive cleanup, or outbound actions.

Produce the requested record plan, import-ready data, updates, or Growth Ops report. Pass
the gate when totals reconcile, freshness is stated, and no CRM data was invented.
If no connection is approved, do not imply that external records were changed.
