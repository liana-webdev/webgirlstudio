# WGS Client Delivery

Read the Source Manifest, Client Delivery document, project brief, content status,
dailies, and QA record.

1. Confirm decision owners, scope, milestones, dependencies, and approval criteria.
2. State the decision needed and show only relevant evidence.
3. Record approved, testing, blocked, creative concern, and next action.
4. Separate preference feedback from task failure and scope change.
5. Complete QA, access, editing guidance, ownership, limitations, and launch approval.
6. Record only real post-launch results with measurement dates.

Pass the gate when approvals and unknowns are explicit and no contract terms are invented.
