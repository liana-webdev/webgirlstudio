# WGS Outreach

1. Run `wgs-source-check` and read `docs/wgs/06-growth/WGS-OUTREACH-PROTOCOL.md`.
2. Confirm the prospect, source URLs, jurisdiction, and permitted research scope.
3. Separate observed facts from inferences and unknowns.
4. Select one consequential, supportable problem rather than producing a generic audit.
5. Connect the problem to a plausible business effect without inventing metrics.
6. Draft a concise, specific message in WGS voice with one low-friction next step.
7. Record research date, evidence, status, owner, and next action in the approved system.
8. Stop at a draft unless the user explicitly authorizes external sending.

Produce an evidence note, draft, and follow-up plan. Pass the gate only when every
claim is traceable, privacy is respected, and sending remains separately approved.
Reject scraped personal data, fabricated familiarity, false urgency, and unsupported
performance or revenue promises.
