# WGS Creative Studio Operating System

Use `00-start-here/WGS-HOW-WE-WORK.md` as the stage model. This document defines
the creative decision record within it.

## Creative inputs

- Approved brief and strategy
- Real content and proof
- Desired and undesired feelings
- Technical, timing, budget, accessibility, and content constraints
- Reference provenance: what is being borrowed and why

## Required decision records

- Three meaningfully distinct territories before convergence
- Territory selection with reasons and rejected risks
- Previs covering structure, motion, and responsive transformation
- Look-development tests using real content
- Dailies notes during production
- Final QA and postmortem

## Review questions

- Does the idea express the strategy or merely decorate it?
- Is the visual system recognisable without sacrificing comprehension?
- Which element carries the distinctive idea?
- What feels generic, trend-derived, or AI-generated?
- Can it be built cleanly at the required performance level?
- What disappears or changes on mobile and reduced motion?
- What should be removed?

## Gate

Do not move from styleframe to production until content hierarchy, responsive
behaviour, interaction intent, and technical feasibility are understood.
