and development for artists, cultural brands, creative industries, service
businesses, and growing founders. The site positions websites as professional
business systems rather than low-involvement template production.

## Why WGS does not sell $300 websites

WGS uses AI because it is useful: it accelerates research, alternatives,
production, testing, organisation, development, and repetitive work. Efficiency
does not make the project worth less. A professional website is priced for the
quality of the thinking, decisions, execution, and business problem being solved,
not for hours spent manually pushing pixels.

A proper WGS project still requires understanding the business, market, and
audience; defining the offer and message; planning information architecture and
the user journey; developing creative directions; establishing type, colour,
imagery, and visual language; designing responsive interfaces and interaction;
building required integrations; and testing usability, accessibility,
responsiveness, performance, analytics, forms, and SEO.

AI can increase the number of ideas tested and shorten the route from strategy to
execution. It cannot decide what is appropriate for a specific business, what to
remove or emphasise, how a brand should feel, what a user needs first, or whether
an idea deserves production. Those judgments remain the work.

A $300 website is generally scoped around speed, templates, and minimal
involvement. WGS is scoped as a professional creative and strategic project. The
distinction is not whether AI was used; it is the thinking, specificity,
responsibility, refinement, and execution applied to the result.
