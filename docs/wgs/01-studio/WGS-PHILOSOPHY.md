# WGS Philosophy

WGS makes websites with a pulse: distinctive enough to feel authored and clear
enough to use without explanation.

## Current principles

- Beauty and usability are one system, not competing stages.
- Creative, but never confusing.
- Purpose before decoration; structure before polish.
- Show real work early and make interactive proof easy to reach.
- Preserve a business or artist's emotional language while clarifying the action.
- Use plain customer language and sophisticated internal reasoning.
- Experiment through one controlled idea at a time.
- Avoid AI sameness, ornamental dashboards, fake UI, invented evidence, and
  visual complexity that cannot be implemented responsibly.
- Use AI as production leverage; creative direction, judgment, specificity, and
  responsibility remain the work.

## Non-negotiable truth standard

Independent concepts must be labelled. Client results, testimonials, metrics,
approval, and CRM facts must be real and attributable. Unknown is an acceptable
status; fabricated certainty is not.

## Incomplete

Founder-approved language on sustainability, subcontracting, accessibility
commitments beyond project QA, and ethical client exclusions has not been found.
