# WGS Discovery and Research

## Research frame

Start with the decision the work must support. Gather evidence about the
business, audience, market, competitors, offer, objectives, desired feeling,
constraints, current system, and available proof.

## Evidence labels

Mark notes as one of:

- **Approved fact** — confirmed by the client or authoritative source.
- **Observed evidence** — visible in analytics, interfaces, interviews, or market material.
- **Working hypothesis** — plausible and testable, not yet confirmed.
- **Question** — blocks or could materially change a decision.

## Discovery activities

Use the smallest useful combination of stakeholder interview, current-site
audit, analytics review, enquiry/form review, audience conversations, competitor
mapping, search-result review, content inventory, technical audit, and offer
comparison. Research is not complete because many screenshots were collected.

## Interview practice

Ask for recent examples and actual behaviour: how enquiries arrive, what people
misunderstand, why work is won or lost, what must never change, and what evidence
exists. Avoid leading questions that merely confirm the proposed direction.

## Output

Record sources, findings, contradictions, hypotheses, and implications in
`01-research.md`. Promote only approved decisions into `02-strategy.md`.
