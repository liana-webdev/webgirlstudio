# WGS Outreach Protocol

## Source and permission

Use current public business information and the commercial source of truth. Do
not use private contact data from unrelated projects. Sending outreach is an
external action and requires explicit authorization for the recipients and final
message.

## Workflow

1. Select a prospect from the approved CRM pipeline.
2. Verify business, contact route, relevance, and current site state.
3. Record sourced observations, not assumptions about revenue or intent.
4. Identify one material problem WGS can credibly address.
5. Draft a short message with specific evidence, relevance, and one next step.
6. Check tone, factual accuracy, duplication, suppression/lost status, and timing.
7. Log the authorized send and follow-up date in Google Sheets.

## Message standard

Personalised means the insight is specific, not that a template includes a name.
Avoid false familiarity, manipulative urgency, unsolicited attachments, inflated
claims, or invented audits. Do not promise outcomes without evidence.
