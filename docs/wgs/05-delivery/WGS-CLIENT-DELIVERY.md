# WGS Client Delivery

## Delivery record

Maintain one approved brief, one decision log, one content-status record, and one
QA record per serious project. Identify who can approve scope, content, design,
and launch. Do not infer approval from silence.

## Stages

1. Confirm scope, responsibilities, milestones, dependencies, and success evidence.
2. Complete discovery and record unknowns.
3. Approve the brief before territory development.
4. Present territories with rationale, implications, and risks.
5. Approve structure/previs before high-fidelity production.
6. Run dailies and record changes that affect scope or strategy.
7. Complete content, integration, QA, and launch readiness.
8. Handoff access, editing guidance, ownership, and known limitations.
9. Record real results after an appropriate measurement period.

## Client communication

Lead with the decision required, show relevant evidence, and name consequences.
Separate preference feedback from task failure. Never expose internal speculation
as a client fact.

## Incomplete

No approved contract, payment schedule, revision allowance, client portal,
handoff warranty, or maintenance policy was found.
